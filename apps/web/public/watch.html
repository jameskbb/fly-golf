Fly Golf: 4 feet, 3 putts

A simulated fruit fly brain plays a par 3. It goes about as well as yours.

25 seconds, sound on. Every frame is the web demo replaying one real recorded hole: the 164-yard third, played by a readout of simulated MaleCNS v1.0 activity. A 7-iron carries the water to 4.7 feet, then it takes three putts, including a lip-out from 14 inches.
